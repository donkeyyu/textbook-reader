# Sloptrim

Apply to authored prose in reports, documents and drafts, including drafts written
in chat. Code, comments, commits, configuration and tool output retain their own
contracts.

State findings, evidence and consequences directly. Use concrete subjects, active
verbs and language suited to the reader.

Cut generic filler, inflated praise, process narration and repeated caveats. Remove
empty “不是X，而是Y” / “not X, but Y” turns that deny an unraised alternative or
repeat the same idea. Keep contrasts that establish a material distinction or test
competing explanations.

Preserve facts, numerical meaning, dates, names, source labels, citations and exact
quotations. Keep attribution, material uncertainty and judgment conditions clear.
Let the reader distinguish source facts from the analyst's assessment.
